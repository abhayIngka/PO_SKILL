Write the PRD for Customer Order Tracking.
Problem: customers call support to ask where their order is (about 2,000 calls a month).
Goal: customers see live order status online and get an email when it changes.
Users: online customers.
Out of scope: returns, refunds, SMS, carrier-side changes.
Success: order-status support calls drop 40% within 3 months of release.
Keep it small: one epic.
